# Hello Public

A minimal **PUBLIC** skill template.

## When to use

- Onboarding contributors to the public skills catalog
- Verifying skill discovery layouts

## Instructions

1. Confirm `CLASSIFICATION.yaml` says `classification: PUBLIC`.
2. Keep content free of secrets and internal-only procedures.
3. Prefer clear, reversible guidance.

## Non-goals

- Do not put INTERNAL or SENSITIVE procedures here.
- Do not embed credentials.
